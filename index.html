05 / CLIENT CARE

AFTERCARE MATTERS.

Your tattoo or piercing is more than body art. Proper aftercare supports healing and helps protect your results. Always follow the instructions provided by your artist.

TATTOO AFTERCARE

01

KEEP IT CLEAN

Wash your hands before touching your tattoo. When instructed to remove the dressing, gently clean the area with lukewarm water and mild fragrance-free soap. Pat dry with a clean disposable paper towel.

02

MOISTURIZE LIGHTLY

Apply a thin layer of the recommended fragrance-free moisturizer after cleaning, once your artist advises you to do so. Avoid heavy layers of lotion.

03

DO NOT SCRATCH

Some itching and peeling can occur during healing. Do not scratch, pick scabs or remove peeling skin. Allow the tattoo to heal naturally.

04

PROTECT YOUR TATTOO

Avoid swimming, soaking in baths, direct sunlight and unnecessary friction while your tattoo heals. Wear clean, loose-fitting clothing.

PIERCING AFTERCARE

01

CLEAN GENTLY

For most external piercings, use sterile 0.9% saline wound wash as directed. Avoid harsh chemicals, alcohol and hydrogen peroxide.

02

AVOID TOUCHING

Always wash your hands before handling your piercing. Do not twist or rotate the jewelry unnecessarily.

03

PREVENT IRRITATION

Avoid pressure, friction and unnecessary contact with the piercing. Keep bedding and anything touching the area clean.

04

ALLOW TIME TO HEAL

Healing time depends on the piercing location. Follow the specific instructions provided for your piercing. Do not change jewelry prematurely.

WHEN TO SEEK MEDICAL HELP

Seek medical advice if you experience worsening redness, swelling or pain, spreading warmth, pus, fever or other concerning symptoms. Seek urgent care for severe symptoms or difficulty breathing or swallowing after a piercing. Do not remove jewelry from a suspected infected piercing unless a healthcare professional advises you to do so.

06 / GOOD TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Mini tattoos start from KSh 500. Custom tattoo prices depend on size, design complexity and placement. Contact us for a quotation.

Do you offer mobile tattoo services?

Yes! Deko Tattoos offers mobile appointments in selected areas of Nairobi and Thika, subject to availability and a suitable hygienic working environment.

How long does a tattoo take to heal?

The surface of a tattoo commonly heals within approximately 2 to 4 weeks. Deeper healing may take longer. Healing varies between individuals.

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

Tattooing can cause discomfort. The level of pain varies depending on the body placement, duration and individual sensitivity.

Can I bring my own tattoo design?

Absolutely! You can share your design ideas or reference pictures. We can discuss how to adapt your idea into a suitable tattoo.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill out our online booking form. Your appointment request will be sent directly to our WhatsApp. We will then discuss availability, design details and pricing.

Do you offer body piercings?

Yes. Contact Deko Tattoos to discuss available piercing services, pricing, placement and aftercare.
